Research

I work in probability theory, mainly in stochastic geometry and in distributional approximation via Stein’s method and Malliavin calculus. I study spatial random structures such as random geometric graphs, random tessellations and birth–growth models, with a particular interest in functionals that exhibit long-range dependence.

Besides Gaussian approximation, non-Gaussian limit theory such as Dickman approximation is a recurring theme in my work. Recent projects concern Gaussian approximation for region-stabilizing functionals, with statistical applications to random forests and to matching-based estimators of average treatment effects, as well as noise sensitivity in continuum percolation and superconcentration for Poisson functionals.
